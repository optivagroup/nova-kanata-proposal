Why composite

Wood was never built for Canadian winters.

Water soaks in, freezes and cracks it. Then the sanding, staining and sealing starts again every spring. Nova Kanata composite is made to skip all of that.

Traditional wood

Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Call the office at (905) 889-7778.

How is composite different from a wood fence?

Our WPC boards combine a 60% natural wood-fibre core with a 360° protective coating. You get the look and feel of real wood without the water damage, cracking, rot and yearly staining that wood needs.

Do I ever need to stain or paint it?

No. Nova Kanata composite is maintenance-free: no painting, no staining, no sealing.

What does the 30-year warranty cover?

The 30-year warranty covers the composite material. Installation is not included in that warranty. Ask our team about the details for your project.

Will it hold up to Canadian winters?

It's designed for exactly that: intense sun, heavy rain, snow and freeze-thaw cycles. The German BASF UK235 anti-aging surface protects against UV and fading.

What colours are available?

Fences come in Charcoal, Grey and Brown. NovaGroove siding comes in Charcoal, Gray and Brown, and NovaFlat in Charcoal and Brown. Visit the showroom to see them in person.

Do you install, and where?

Yes. We supply and professionally install across the Greater Toronto Area.
