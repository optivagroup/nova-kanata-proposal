How a customer moves through the system

  1. Sees the adMeta ads on Facebook and Instagram.
  2. Asks for a quoteThe website and its quote form collect the details.
  3. Gets a reply in a minuteText and email, at any hour. The AI agent starts the conversation.
  4. Your team is alertedDetails and conversation arrive together. Your team sends the quote.
  5. Follow-up runs itselfIf they go quiet, texts and emails go out. A report follows every two weeks.

Nova Kanata keeps pricing, quoting, closing the sale and delivery. The system hands your team a customer who is already qualified.
